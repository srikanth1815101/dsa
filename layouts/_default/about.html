About CSRGO DSA

To make coding interview preparation simple, structured, and accessible for every developer.

Who We Are

CSRGO DSA is a focused platform built to help developers prepare for technical interviews through structured Data Structures and Algorithms practice.

The platform contains a curated collection of interview-style problems organized into clear learning paths — from fundamentals to advanced topics. Each problem is designed to strengthen core problem-solving skills and cover common patterns used in real software engineering interviews.

CSRGO DSA is intentionally simple. There are no accounts, no complex dashboards, and no distractions. The goal is to provide a clean and efficient space where developers can focus entirely on practicing and improving their algorithmic thinking.

Whether you are preparing for your first coding interview or revising key concepts before an interview, CSRGO DSA aims to make structured practice simple and accessible.
