This Privacy Policy describes how CSRGO DSA ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our website at [dsa.csrgo.com](https://dsa.csrgo.com/) (the "Site").

## 1. No Personal Information Collected

We want to be transparent: **We do not collect any personal information from you.**

- **No Accounts:** We do not have user accounts, login systems, or signup forms.
- **No Direct Collection:** We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.
- **No Analytics:** We do not use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Hotjar, or any analytics or behavioral tracking service.
- **No Advertising:** We do not serve advertisements or work with any ad networks.

## 2. Data Stored Locally in Your Browser

CSRGO DSA uses your browser's **LocalStorage** and **IndexedDB** to store data **entirely on your device**:

- **Problem Progress:** Which problems you've solved, attempted, bookmarked, or marked for revision.
- **Interview Sessions:** Timed interview session history, configurations, and performance results.
- **Personal Notes:** Notes you write for individual problems and interview reflections.
- **Preferences:** Theme settings (light/dark mode) and UI filter states.

This data **never leaves your device**. It is not transmitted to any server, API, or third party. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, this data will be lost unless you export it first using the tools on your [Profile](/profile/) page.

## 3. Hosting and Technology

CSRGO DSA is a static website hosted on **Netlify**. As part of standard hosting, Netlify may collect basic server logs (IP addresses, access timestamps). This is governed by [Netlify's Privacy Policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/).

We load the following external resources for functionality:

- **Google Fonts** (Inter, Fira Code) — subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Tailwind CSS CDN** — a CSS framework, no data collection
- **Lucide Icons CDN** — an icon library, no data collection
- **Font Awesome CDN** — subject to [Font Awesome's Privacy Policy](https://fontawesome.com/privacy)

## 4. Cookies

CSRGO DSA does **not** use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or any third-party cookies.

We use only essential browser storage (LocalStorage, IndexedDB) to preserve your local progress and preferences. For full details, see our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/).

## 5. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally in your browser:

- **No server-side breaches** are possible — we simply don't store your data.
- **You control your data** — export, import, or delete it anytime via the [Profile](/profile/) page.
- **No third-party access** — only you (and anyone with physical access to your device) can view your data.
- **No data sharing** — we never sell, rent, or share any user data.

## 6. Data Retention

We do not retain any user data on our servers. All data exists solely in your browser and persists only as long as you choose. You may delete it at any time via:

- The **Clear Data** option on your [Profile](/profile/) page
- Your browser's settings to clear site data for `dsa.csrgo.com`
- Your browser's DevTools → Application → Clear Storage

## 7. Children's Privacy

CSRGO DSA is an educational platform. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Since no data is stored on our servers and no accounts are created, there is no risk of personal data collection from minors.

## 8. International Users

CSRGO DSA is accessible worldwide. Since we do not collect or process personal data on any server, there are no cross-border data transfers. All data remains in your local browser regardless of your location.

## 9. Your Rights

Since we collect no personal data, traditional data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability) under GDPR, CCPA, and similar regulations are inherently satisfied — there is no personal data held by us.

You always retain full control over your locally stored data through the functions on your [Profile](/profile/) page.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Since we don't collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly — we recommend checking this page periodically.

Your continued use of CSRGO DSA after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
