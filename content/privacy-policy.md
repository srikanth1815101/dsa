**Last Updated: March 6, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how CSRGO DSA ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our website at https://dsa.csrgo.com/ (the "Site").

## 1. No Personal Information Collected

We want to be transparent: **We do not collect any personal information from you.**

*   **No Accounts:** We do not have user accounts, login systems, or signup forms.
*   **No Comments:** We do not have a commenting system that requires you to submit personal details.
*   **No Direct Collection:** We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.
*   **No Analytics:** We do not use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or any analytics or behavioral tracking service.

## 2. Data Stored Locally in Your Browser

CSRGO DSA uses your browser's **LocalStorage** and **IndexedDB** to store data **entirely on your device**:

*   **Problem Progress:** Which problems you've solved, attempted, bookmarked, or marked for revision.
*   **Interview Sessions:** Timed interview session history, configurations, and performance results.
*   **Personal Notes:** Notes you write for individual problems and interview reflections.
*   **Preferences:** Theme settings (light/dark mode) and UI filter states.

This data **never leaves your device**. It is not transmitted to any server, API, or third party. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, this data will be lost unless you export it first using the tools on your [Profile](/profile/) page.

## 3. Hosting and Technology (Netlify)

This Site is a static website hosted on **Netlify**.

*   **Netlify:** As our hosting provider, Netlify may collect access logs containing your IP address to ensure the security and reliability of their service. This is standard technical practice for all websites. You can read Netlify's Privacy Policy here: [https://www.netlify.com/privacy/](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/)

We also load the following external resources for functionality:

*   **Google Fonts** (Inter, Fira Code) — subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
*   **Tailwind CSS CDN** — a CSS framework with no data collection
*   **Lucide Icons CDN** — an icon library with no data collection
*   **Font Awesome CDN** — subject to [Font Awesome's Privacy Policy](https://fontawesome.com/privacy)

## 4. Cookies

**We do not use first-party cookies to track your personal identity.** We do not have user accounts, shopping carts, or login sessions that require cookies.

We use only essential browser storage mechanisms (LocalStorage, IndexedDB) to preserve your local progress and preferences. For full details, see our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/).

## 5. Advertising

We do not currently serve any advertisements. However, we reserve the right to introduce advertising in the future to support the maintenance of this Site. If and when we implement advertising, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 6. Data Security

Since all user data is stored locally in your browser:

*   **No server-side breaches** are possible — we simply don't store your data on any server.
*   **You control your data** — export, import, or delete it anytime via the [Profile](/profile/) page.
*   **No third-party access** — only you (and anyone with physical access to your device) can view your locally stored data.

## 7. Children's Privacy

CSRGO DSA is an educational platform. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is stored on our servers and no accounts are created, there is no risk of personal data collection from minors.

## 8. External Links

Our Site contains links to external websites that are not operated by us. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that third party's site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions of every site you visit. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites, products, or services.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
