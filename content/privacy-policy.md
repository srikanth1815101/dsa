CSRGO DSA ("we", "our", or "the platform") respects your privacy. This website is designed as a lightweight educational platform and does not require user accounts or personal data for normal use.

## 1. Information We Collect

CSRGO DSA does not collect personal information during normal usage of the platform.

### Local Browser Storage

Some features store data locally in your browser using technologies such as Local Storage. This may include:

- Problem completion status
- Personal notes created by users
- Interview practice history
- Platform preferences

This information remains on your device and is **not transmitted to our servers**.

If you clear browser data or uninstall the application, this information may be permanently deleted.

### Contact Form

The website may provide an optional contact form for feedback, bug reports, or suggestions.

If you choose to submit a message, you may voluntarily provide:

- Name
- Email address
- Message content

This information is used only to respond to your message.

Providing this information is completely optional.
