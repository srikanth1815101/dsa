# How It Works

Welcome to the ultimate DSA preparation platform. We've designed a workflow that mirrors real-world interview pressure while providing the tools you need to track and improve your skills effectively.

## 1. Interview Sessions

The core of our platform is the **Interview Session**. Instead of solving problems in isolation, you create a dedicated session—simulating a real coding round.

- **Modes**: Choose "Quick" for a rapid drill or "Custom" to target specific companies or topics.
- **Timer**: Every session runs against a clock. This builds the muscle memory needed to perform under time constraints.
- **Problem Set**: We curate a balanced mix of problems (Easy, Medium, Hard) based on your chosen difficulty profile.

## 2. Tracking Your Progress

We believe in "Active Recall" over passive practice.

- **Solved**: Mark a problem as solved only when you pass all test cases.
- **Attempted**: Mark as attempted if you developed a logic but ran out of time or hit edge case errors.
- **Skipped**: Use this for problems you consciously decided to pass on.

## 3. Review & Reflection

Growth happens during review. After every session:

- **Analyze**: Check your time-per-problem and accuracy.
- **Reflect**: Use the "Reflection Note" feature to jot down what went wrong (e.g., "Forgot DP base case" or "Struggled with Graph BFS").
- **Revision**: Flag tough problems for later revision using the bookmark feature.

## 4. The "Gold Standard" Philosophy

Our goal isn't just to help you solve 100 problems, but to help you **master the patterns**.

> "Amateurs practice until they get it right. Professionals practice until they can't get it wrong."

Consistent use of our **Interview Mode** ensures that when you step into a real interview, it feels just like another practice session.
