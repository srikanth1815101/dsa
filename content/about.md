# Our Mission

**CSRGO DSA** was born from a simple observation: most developers practice coding in a way that doesn't prepare them for the actual interview. They solve problems leisurely, peek at solutions too early, and rarely track their time.

We exist to bridge that gap.

## The Vision

We are building a platform that treats interview preparation like **athletic training**. 

- **High Intensity**: Timed, focused sessions.
- **Data Driven**: Metrics on speed, accuracy, and topic strength.
- **Aesthetic Focus**: A beautiful, distraction-free environment that you actually *want* to use.

## Community First

This project is open and community-driven. We believe that top-tier preparation tools should be accessible to everyone who has the drive to succeed.
